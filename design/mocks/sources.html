Voice

Two voices, never mixed

The narrator is the voice of void's founder, synthesised with their consent. Until that voice is ready, a computer voice stands in, and this page names it. In week 32 the narrator is the stand-in, Kokoro af_aoede at 0.88 speed.

Every quotation, headline and period document is read by a second computer voice, Kokoro am_michael at 0.86, so the programme's words and the record's words never share a voice. On screen, a dot at the end of the lower third grows a label when a synthesised or computer voice starts.
