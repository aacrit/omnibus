# 2026-11-22: T7 does not touch `studio/graphics/render.py` (builder, coordinating with T9c)

Ticket 4 left the title unrendered in `render_episode` (its drawn shot, `kind: "drawn"`, is skipped by `graphics_shots`, which only walks `kind: "graphic"` shots; `V2_RENDERERS` has no title entry). T9c (branch `t9c/render-35`, open as PR #45) is concurrently wiring a basic title renderer into `V2_RENDERERS`, reusing the v1 title-macro renderer, so issue 35 can render on Actions without waiting on this ticket.

This ticket's two deliverables - `studio.news.title` (the recompositor: pure functions plus a small CLI, `python -m studio.news.title <episode dir> [--check|--write]`) and `title_lines_checks` (`studio/news/lints.py`'s `title-lines-from-rundown`) - are deliberately independent of `studio/graphics/render.py`. The lint is wired into `lints.checks()`, which `render.build_plan` already journals for every screenplay (see that module's own docstring), so it runs ahead of any render regardless of which function in `studio/graphics/render.py` eventually draws the title; nothing here needed a new call site in the render driver, and nothing here conflicts with T9c's.

Once T9c's `V2_RENDERERS` entry merges, the clean follow-up (left to whichever ticket touches it next, so as not to fork the graphics driver from two branches at once) is for that renderer to build its `TitleMacroSpec` from `title.recompose(rundown, echo_pick)` directly rather than trusting the screenplay's own `shows.title_lines`/`week_line` as authored - the lint would then be a second, independent guarantee (catching a stale screenplay before a render even starts) rather than the only one.
