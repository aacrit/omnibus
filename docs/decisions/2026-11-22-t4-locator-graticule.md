# 2026-11-22: T4, the locator draws a graticule, not a traced coastline (builder, from show bible v2 section i)

Section i asks for "Natural Earth and GeoNames in cream line on the field... no globe, no 3D, no relief." void document's own map engine (`studio/engine/maps.py`, transplanted into this repo already) draws exactly that, but only against Natural Earth's vector GeoJSON, fetched through `--geodata DIR` at render time on that engine's own callers. Those files are a data snapshot - Agentique CLAUDE.md rule 8 ("no generated files, binaries, media, or data snapshots in git") - so they are never committed here, and no step in `episode-draft.yml` fetches them today.

Rather than hold ticket 4 on wiring a Natural Earth fetch into the graphics job (a second CI job, a licence credit line, a cache key, a new failure mode, for a locator that the bible itself says needs no relief or globe), this ships the honest half now: `layout.locator_point` is a real plate-carree projection of a place's own cited lon/lat, not a guessed fractional position, and `layout.graticule_lines` draws real meridians and parallels at the same projection. What is missing is the shoreline itself; the field is a grid, not a silhouette of any country. `layout.LOCATOR_BOX`'s own comment states this plainly, and `clean.assert_no_bias_tokens` plus the "no globe, no 3D, no relief" phrasing both hold regardless.

A follow-up ticket that wants the coastline back has a clear path: run `studio.engine.maps` against Natural Earth fetched fresh in the graphics job (mirroring how `capture.yml` already fetches other third-party data at run time, never committing it), and feed its output image into `locator_html` as a background layer under the existing grid/label/leader markup, which does not need to change. Nothing in this ticket's data model (`LocatorSpec`, `shot["locator"]`) forecloses that.
