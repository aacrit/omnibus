# 2026-11-13: E9i: the engine reads omnibus's own ledgers

On a news show only (a document film plans as before). **Licences.** `studio/news/profile.py` LICENCES maps every rights-ledger id (`licences.mjs` ALLOWED_IDS) to the engine's allowlist semantics: pd-us-federal, pd-age, pd-dedication, cc0-1.0, own-property, eu-reuse-credit and cc-by-* are allowed; cc-by-sa-* is share-alike, allowed only under the screenplay's `share_alike` declaration (the episode is CC BY-SA 4.0). **Graphics.** A ledger reference with `"format": "e6-graphics"` reads E6's `graphics-ledger.json` through `studio/news/graphics_ledger.py`: file from output_path's bare name, fps 30 and straight alpha (E6's one encode), shows_bodies false, CC BY-SA 4.0, and content_date from data_ref (the rundown entry E6 typesets, else the week record; echo-pick alone stays undated, so cw-4 is refused if cited); it replaces the writer's interim `ledgers/graphics-e4b.json`. E6 may state any of those fields itself and wins. **Vision** (`episodes/<n>/vision.json`, schema `omnibus-vision/1`, `studio/news/vision_manifest.py`): E10 writes shows_bodies and located people per item, bound to the file's sha256; the ledger wins and a contradiction is refused; a picture with no reading is a warning under --draft (its no-bodies and people checks too) and refused in a final render (`vision-manifest`). **Voicing** (`episodes/<n>/audio/voice.json`, the A57 manifest, schema `omnibus-voicing/1`, `studio/news/voicing_manifest.py`): per line the cast voice, wav, sha256, text_sha256, dur_s and word timings; the plan reads each line's take and words from it; refused until E7 writes it, in --draft too. **Sound and credits.** A rights-ledger `audio: {mode: muted}` is the clip's sound (`mute`, with its reason); a shot stating `own` is refused. Each aired asset's `disclosure` (disputes.json credit_disclosures) is a Disclosures block in the credits and `credit_disclosures` in sources.json. **Check.** `python -m studio.engine.render <film> --check [--draft]` runs every pre-flight and plan-time lint without rendering; the manifests' refusals are deferred to the end so it names everything else first.
