# 2026-11-21: T5, kantei.go.jp's terms are PDL1.0, whose clause 1.7 grants CC BY 4.0; refused until the CoS signs (builder, from show bible v2 section n)

**Finding.** Section n names kantei.go.jp as "CC BY 4.0". Its copyright policy (last updated 31 March 2025; the Japanese text prevails) actually applies the Digital Agency's Public Data License 1.0. PDL1.0 clause 1.7 says the rules are compatible with CC BY 4.0 and that the State permits use under CC BY ("利用者がCC BYに従って利用することを許諾します"). So the ledger records `cc-by-4.0` with that grant as its basis, and the three pages (the English policy, the Japanese original and PDL1.0) are pinned by sha256 in `state-terms.mjs`. **Held for the CoS.** The builder's reading is not a review, so all three entries are signed `builder` and every kantei item is refused ("not signed by a checking role") until the CoS changes `reviewed.by` to `cos` on them. That is a one-line edit per entry, and the tests already cover the signed path. **Rules from the policy.** The credit is the policy's form for edited content, "Created by editing "<title>" (Official Website of the Prime Minister's Office of Japan) (<url>)", because a rostrum tour edits the still. A picture whose caption states anyone's rights (©, a photographer, Kyodo, Jiji, a pool) falls under other terms and is refused, as is a logo or symbol mark (PDL1.0 1.4). A still must name its picture. kantei's videos are YouTube embeds and are never taken, so the adapter takes footage only from a video file on kantei's own host, and none exists today.
