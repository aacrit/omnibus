# 2026-11-21: T5, record_kind: from the source's own title or a checking role's review; anything else is unknown and airs muted (builder, from show bible v2 sections e and n)

Every new footage item in the ledger states `record_kind` (one of the ten in section n) and `record_kind_evidence`. **Where the kind comes from.** An adapter states it only where its source's own record says it unmistakably, read from the title: "Press conference ..." is `news_conference`, "Remarks by ..." is `remarks`, "Press Briefing ..." is `briefing`, "Address to ..." is `address` (`recordKindFromTitle`; the Kremlin and federal-page adapters). A meeting, a visit or an interview is never read from its title, because an interview may be another crew's film. Otherwise a pin may carry `record_kind: { kind, by, at, why }`, admitted only when signed by a checking role (verifier, cos or founder) with a sentence on what was seen. A builder's or sourcer's word is refused, the same rule as the marks review. A review that contradicts the source's own reading refuses the item. With neither, the kind is `unknown`. **Fail-closed.** Footage whose kind is not one of the seven that may feed SOT (produced, promo, unknown) is ledgered with `audio: { mode: muted, reason }` unless its pin already mutes it, so the screenplay's existing sound-role check (`studio/news/schema.py`: a clip the ledger airs muted is never sot or nat) keeps it off SOT and nat. This applies to the SSU strike video until a checking role reviews it. The `record-kind` rights lint blocks footage with no kind, a non-SOT kind not muted, and an unsigned review. The issue 33 ledger, written before v2, is frozen (`V1_LEDGER_ISSUES`), like T1's V1 path. The screenplay-side `sot-raw-source` lint (an sot line's asset has a SOT kind) is T6's, and reads this field.
