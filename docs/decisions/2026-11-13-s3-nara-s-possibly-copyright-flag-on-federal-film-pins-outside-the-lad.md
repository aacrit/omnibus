# 2026-11-13: S3: NARA's possibly-copyright flag on federal film; pins outside the ladder; date records (supersedes the E5d NARA line above for Signal Corps items)

Coordinator's sourcing ruling for the Echo (issue 33): federal-agency films that NARA flags conservatively may pass only with the federal creator proven on the record. **Flag.** NARA marks whole federal film series (Signal Corps 111-ADC, Marine Corps 127-R) "Restricted - Possibly" with Copyright as a blanket caution. Such a record passes as pd-us-federal only when Copyright is its only specific restriction, the record names a federal maker that existed when the film was made (a later custodian never counts), it sits in a federal record group (not a donated collection), and it names no commercial newsreel or studio; its basis and evidence say the flag was there. "Undetermined" never passes, and MCA/Universal newsreel items stay refused (collection, commercial newsreel, no federal maker). **Pins.** A candidate-file pin on an adapter no ladder step names was silently dropped; it now joins its request at its stated tier and window (both required), before later tiers, leaving the ladder's own order alone. A pin that states a fit keeps no undated exemption: its date must be on the record and inside the window. **Dates.** A pin's `date_record: { adapter, ref }` names a second catalogue record of the same item; the fetcher takes that record's full-day date only inside the source's own date (it refines, never moves it) and keeps the record as `evidence_also`. First use: the four Abbie Rowe Armory stills, dated 29 March 1952 by NARA 338959010, 338959024, 338959044 and 338959048 (Truman Library accessions 73-3866, 73-3872, 73-3881, 73-3883).
