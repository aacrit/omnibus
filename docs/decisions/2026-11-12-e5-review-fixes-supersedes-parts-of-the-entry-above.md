# 2026-11-12: E5 review fixes (supersedes parts of the entry above)

On review of PR #12. A picture for a news part (cold open, The Week, The Weight, The Margins) needs a known publication time, its source's own upload or post time, on or before the part's horizon: the Komyshuvakha video (taken 2 Nov, uploaded 5 Nov) is dropped to its tier 4 locator map, so issue 33 holds 13 of 53. Federal pages read credits element by element to each caption's end, never to a full stop, and refuse agency, stock and pool credits in their common forms ("AP Photo/X", "Photo: Reuters/X", "Image: Getty Images", "X/Getty Images", "Photo by Reuters", "(Reuters)"), whatever federal office stands beside them; a still needs a positive federal credit ("Official White House Photo by"); an explicit media file must be named on its page, XML pages included. NASA: a name after "NASA/" passes only when the record's own photographer or secondary_creator field names the same person, and Aerojet, Rocketdyne, Jacobs, Hopkins, State, Research, Propulsion and Canyon mark an organisation. Excluding the Jet magazine page is a policy call (outlet pages are never shown), not a licence finding.
