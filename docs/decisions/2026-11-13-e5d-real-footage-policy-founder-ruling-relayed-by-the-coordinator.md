# 2026-11-13: E5d: real footage policy (founder ruling, relayed by the coordinator)

Founder ruling: the show is real news with footage first, zero AI pixels, and the rights rules widen by three classes only. **VOA.** Voice of America's own material is a US federal work: `voanews.com` leaves the denied hosts and "VOA" / "Voice of America" leave the outlet credits. The `voa` adapter reads one video page (www.voanews.com/a/<id>.html) and VOA's Terms of Use (www.voanews.com/p/5338.html, quoted: "All text, audio and video material produced exclusively by the Voice of America is in the public domain"); the page must name VOA and credit no one outside it (an AP, Reuters, AFP or Getty credit refuses it, since VOA only licenses their material), and the file is the largest mp4 the page offers on its video host. The fetcher's hosts gain `www.voanews.com` and `voa-video-ns.akamaized.net`. On-screen agency marks inside a VOA package are the vision pass's to find. **editorial-use.** Footage an institution publishes free for news or editorial use enters with a `terms` record (the terms page, its quoted text, the credit it requires, its restrictions, an empty list meaning none); terms that charge a fee, require prior permission or a licence, or bar news, online or broadcast use refuse it (`editorialTermsProblems`). Such an item keeps its own terms and is not relicensed under the episode's CC BY-SA 4.0. Checked on 2026-11-13: the UN Audiovisual Library's terms state "service and licensing fees" for video, so UN Web TV and the AV Library stay on the denied hosts; the Ukrainian President's site and dsns.gov.ua answer 403. **fair-use-excerpt.** An outlet's clip, only as evidence of coverage in The Weight beside the board, never as illustration; the only class that may carry an outlet's material (agencies stay refused). Blocking lint `fair-use-excerpt`: footage or sound; beat `weight-*`; at most 10 s and 2% of its source; at most 4 and 45 s per episode; outlet named on screen; its own sound, unmuted; `no_recut`; a written rationale (120 characters or more); `no_youtube: true` (the episode carrying it is not uploaded to YouTube); balanced across void news's ladder (as many clips left of centre as right, each naming its counterpart on the other side). Open question for the founder: downloading an outlet's clip from YouTube is against YouTube's terms, which the values rule out (scraping against terms); sourcing passes take clips only from an outlet's own page offering the file. **NARA moving images.** The `nara` adapter takes the record's MP4 for a footage request, and a federal body named as creator whose dates begin after the record was made is its custodian, not its author (the Eisenhower Library, from 2011, did not film "Campaign: 1952"). NARA marks Army Signal Corps items and the MCA/Universal newsreels "Restricted - Possibly" (copyright), which stays a refusal. **Candidate pins.** Sourcing passes write `episodes/<issue>/candidates/<source>.json` (`schema: omnibus.asset-candidates/1`, `issue`, `source`, `pins: { <request id>: [pin] }`), merged after `asset-pins.json` by `rights.mjs resolve`; each pin is an asset-pins pin plus a required `fit: { beat, why, in_s?, out_s?, horizon? }` and, for an excerpt, `fair_use`. The fit travels into the ledger item and ASSETS.md's "Footage fit" section.
