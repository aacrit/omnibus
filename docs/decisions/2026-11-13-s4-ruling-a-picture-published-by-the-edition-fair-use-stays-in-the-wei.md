# 2026-11-13: S4: ruling A (picture published by the edition), fair use stays in The Weight, war.gov and state.gov

Founder rulings of 2026-11-13, relayed by the coordinator. **Ruling A.** In The Week, a picture or clip of a day's own event may be published any time up to the edition cutoff (the rundown's `as_of`, 2026-11-08T22:16:50Z), not only by that day's print time; facts and narration stay at the day's horizon. The rule is `picture_published_by_edition`: the item's event date is a full day inside the day's window (on or before the horizon's day) and its publication is at or before the cutoff. `resolve` writes the cutoff into `assets.json` (`edition_cutoff`) and, for a Week day only, runs its DVIDS search to the cutoff (the candidate keeps the day's own window); the fetcher admits such an item and records `published_by_edition` and a `dot_label` ("FILMED 2 NOV 2026 · PUBLISHED 5 NOV 2026") on its ledger item. The E3 lint `picture_published_by_edition` (studio/news/lints.py) blocks a news picture published after its part's horizon unless it is in The Week, its event is the day's, it was published by the cutoff, and both its ledger item and its shot carry that label. The cold open, The Weight and The Margins keep the part's horizon (theirs is the cutoff already). A pin may also carry its own `dot_label` (a sourcing pass's requirement), which the fetcher records the same way. **Ruling B.** Fair-use excerpts stay in The Weight only; no scope change (the existing lint already refuses one elsewhere). **Hosts.** The fetcher's federal extension gains `war.gov` (the Pentagon's own host under the Department of War name; `media.defense.gov` was already reachable through `defense.gov`) and `state.gov`, each with its body in `adapters/gov.mjs` and the same proof rule as every federal page (it names its body and credits no one outside it; a "State Department photo by" or "Department of War" credit is the body's own).
