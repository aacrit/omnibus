# 2026-11-22: T7, the Echo's printed year reads the echo pick first, the rundown second, the bank id last (builder)

Section d's title prints the Echo's year (`THE ECHO       1945`), but no section o field is named as its source. Three places can carry it by issue 35's own data: `episodes/35/echo-pick.json` states `"year": 1945` directly (T9b's own write-up of the pick); the rundown's `echo` object (`omnibus.rundown/2`) does not carry a `year` field at all in the real issue 35 data, only `id: "yalta-conference-1945"`; and the id's own `-1945` suffix is readable by pattern, when a bank id happens to end in one (not guaranteed - section q's provisional example id, `cuban-missile-crisis`, does not).

`title.echo_year(rundown, echo_pick)` tries the echo pick's `year` first (the record a screenplay actually names in `news.echo_pick`, and the one ticket 8's echo bank is most likely to keep current), then the rundown's own `echo.year` (for a screenplay with no echo pick named, or a future rundown shape that adds the field there instead), then the bank id's trailing `-YYYY` as a last resort. It raises, rather than guessing, when none of the three states a year: a title with no year printed wrong is worse than a lint that fails loudly and names the gap.

This order means the echo pick can override a rundown recomposited from a bank entry whose id lies about its own year (or has none); `studio.news.title`'s recompositor is called with both wherever both are at hand (`lints.title_lines_checks` passes `inputs.get("echo")`; the CLI reads `echo-pick.json` when the episode folder has one), so this is the common path, not the fallback.
