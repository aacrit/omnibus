# 2026-11-13: C7: the weekly capture pool and its candidates

The first capture run that sees a new weekly issue n (the newest snapshot's `weekly_issue`) writes `capture/weekly/<n>/pool.json` (`omnibus.capture-pool/1`) to R2 and a copy to the run's artifact: the week's lead, its seven day leads and its margins, each with the captured items published in its window (a day: the day before to the day after; a margin: around its printed day; the lead: the week) that score at least `capture_at` against the story's own cluster titles and deep-dive article titles, with their times, verdict, media key and evidence keys. The week record is the committed `data/week-<n>.json`, else normalised from the R2 snapshots (schema 1, echo pending); when neither reads, the pool waits for a later run. After the rundown, `node scripts/rights.mjs candidates <n> [--pool r2|artifact|<path>]` maps stories to requests by the beat's `known.cluster_id` (the lead by the rundown's claim clusters), pins ok-verdict footage and stills at the ladder's tier and window (off the ladder: its last tier, ending at the beat's horizon), and writes `episodes/<n>/candidates/capture.json` (`omnibus.asset-candidates/1`), refusing a file `mergeCandidatePins` would refuse. Every fit is automatic (`fit.auto: true`, `fit.why` starting `auto:`), carried into the ledger; the render lint `capture-fit-confirmed` blocks a final render (a warning in a draft or check render) until a Claude session replaces `fit.why` with what the picture shows and sets `fit.confirmed_by`.
