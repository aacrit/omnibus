# 2026-11-21: T5, the transcript ledger class: official, or ours at 98% agreement between two different engines (builder, from show bible v2 section e)

The ledger gains `transcripts[]`: `{ id, source: official|ours, item_id, lang, text, text_sha256, captured_at, span? }`. **official** is the state organ's own published text, on its own host (a federal host, kremlin.ru or kantei.go.jp), with its `url`, its `publisher` and, for a translation, `translated_from`. It carries no ASR fields. **ours** carries exactly two engines' texts `{ engine, model, text }` from two different engines (one engine run twice is one engine), and its `text` is the first engine's, verbatim. The `transcript-class` lint computes the word agreement again from the two texts (1 minus the word edit distance over the longer text's words, after lower-casing and dropping punctuation) and does not trust the stored `agreement`. It blocks below 0.98, a stored score its texts do not give, a sha that is not its text's, and our transcript of an item whose `record_kind` may not feed SOT. `node scripts/rights.mjs transcript <issue> --item <id> (--official <kremlin page> | --ours <a.json> <b.json> --lang <xx> [--span in,out])` writes one record and regenerates the packet, and writes nothing when the record fails. The Kremlin's English page is the one official reader so far. Running the ASR engines is the pilot's (T9). The `sot-verbatim` match of a bite's words to a transcript span is T6's.
