# 2026-11-22: T7, the sign-on's final clause is derived from the tagline constant, never typed a second time (builder)

The acceptance for this ticket groups three things under one lint - "the title's typed lines must equal the rundown's slugs in rank order, plus the week line and the tagline, character for character" - but the teleprinter itself never prints the tagline (section d's S1 to S4 print only the rundown's lines and the week line; the tagline sits under the name on the lockup card, section a, and is spoken, not typed, at S5). The one place a fixed phrase close to the tagline is typed out in the screenplay at all is the sign-on line: `"This is void omnibus. The week of sixteen November, on the record."` - which ends on the tagline's own clause, `on the record.`, not the tagline's opening half, `The week,`.

Rather than hard-code `"on the record."` a second time inside `SIGNON_TEMPLATE` (and risk it silently drifting from `TAGLINE` if the founder ever changes the pick, section a already records one such change), `studio/news/title.py` derives it: `TAGLINE = "The week, on the record."` stays the one place the founder's words live, and `TAGLINE_CLAUSE = TAGLINE.split(", ", 1)[1]` is substituted into `SIGNON_TEMPLATE`. `title_lines_checks` therefore checks the sign-on's text against a value that is structurally tied to the tagline constant, which is the sense in which this lint can be said to check the tagline "character for character" even though no title line prints it whole. The sign-off (bible h) ends on the same clause and should import `TAGLINE_CLAUSE` from here rather than carry its own copy, when that ticket lands.
