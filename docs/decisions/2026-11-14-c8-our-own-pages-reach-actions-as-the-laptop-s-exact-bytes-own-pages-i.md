# 2026-11-14: C8: our own pages reach Actions as the laptop's exact bytes (own-pages-<issue> release; CoS decision)

**Why exact bytes.** After rights fix 4, a rights-ledger item airs only as the exact bytes its ledger checked (`asset-sha` in the render, and the vision manifest's cuts and marks bound to the same sha256). Another machine's Chromium cannot reproduce a page capture byte for byte. The draft at a8f9243 (run 43532926050) therefore refused all 11 Linux recaptures of void news's own pages. The truth lint stays as it is. **How.** The laptop's 11 captures all match their ledger sha256 (checked by the coordinator). They are published unchanged as prerelease assets of `own-pages-33` (not a `release/*` tag; it targets main), named by their ledger `file`. In the sources job, every item with `licence: own-property` is never fetched and never recaptured. It is placed with `gh release download own-pages-<issue>` (the issue number is validated in `route`; the job's token has `contents: read`) and checked against its ledger sha256 (`scripts/lib/rights/pull.mjs`). A missing or mismatched file refuses that item, with no fallback to a recapture. The sources job no longer installs Chromium or clones void news. **Why a release is acceptable here.** These pages are our own property, and their recaptures were already public in this public repo's stage artifacts. The capture design's refusal of release assets (reports/omnibus-capture-design.md, Storage) stays in force for every third-party item: those come only from their own origins, checked against the ledger. `tests/c8-draft.test.ts` checks that an own-property sha mismatch refuses the item and that nothing is fetched or recaptured.
