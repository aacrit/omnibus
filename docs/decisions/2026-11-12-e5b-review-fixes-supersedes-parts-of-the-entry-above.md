# 2026-11-12: E5b review fixes (supersedes parts of the entry above)

On review of PR #14. The next edition's print time is read from a later printed-edition record (void news's main at capture; its commit is kept as `next_edition_from`), never from the record the page was built from, which cannot hold a later edition; a missing next time fails unless that later record shows the edition is still the latest. A served page may trail its print by at most one hour (week 33: 10 to 23 minutes). The deployment's source commit must equal the edition's commit, its environment must be production, and its address must be the deployment's own (`<first 8 of its id>.void-news.pages.dev`), never a branch or preview alias. The capture applies these rules before it keeps a still, and removes a still it refuses. Fixed and sticky overlays (the site nav, the audio player) are hidden before the page is read and counted; hidden elements are not pictures. An inline drawing outside the site's own header, nav and footer counts as a picture. The Paper's Brief is built from the latest brief, not from the edition (3, 6 and 7 Nov carry an earlier day's Brief: 3 Nov shows 2 Nov's, 6 and 7 Nov show 5 Nov's), so each still records it as a band no tour may rest on. The 4 Nov story pages stay refused, now for the hour cap (served 4 h 54 min after the print). DVIDS: the basis says a US federal government work, since the Coast Guard is in DHS; a "Joint" item needs a credit with a US military rank; any non-empty copyright, restrictions or licence field refuses the item.
