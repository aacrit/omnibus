# 2026-11-13: C6/C7 review follow-ups: bytes, reconcile, retries, dignity excludes

On review of cap/c6-c7. The bucket cap counts everything the capture keeps: `index.overhead_bytes` (at least the index's own size plus a 64 KiB reserve for the heartbeat and pin requests, plus each pool written) joins media and evidence in `storedBytes`. A pool is trimmed to 1 MiB by keeping fewer items a story (12, 8, 5, 3) and refused past it or past the bucket cap. Once a week a run lists what R2 holds under `capture/`, deletes media and evidence objects the index does not name, and sets the overhead from every other object. A held item is fetched again only when the day or bucket cap alone refused it and it has no media. `capture/channels.json` gains a dignity `exclude` list (body-camera footage, casualties, graphic or disturbing material, the dead, a death on camera, injury in detail): a matching item stays in the pool with its reason but is never pinned automatically, and `capture.json` lists it under `excluded`. The workflow creates its issue labels only when they are missing. The rule-10 heartbeat answers 503 by design until `CAPTURE_R2_ENABLED` is set. The Actions budget states the worst case, 2,250 minutes a month, unbilled on a public repo. A pool read from an artifact must match its run's own report (issue, written by that run, built inside it).
