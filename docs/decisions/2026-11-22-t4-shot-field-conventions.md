# 2026-11-22: T4, the four new subtypes' own shot fields, beyond what ticket 1's schema checks (builder, from show bible v2 section i/o)

Ticket 1's schema spine (`studio/news/schema.py`, `graphic_problems`) checks what a `bumper`, `sot_super` or `brief_strip` shot's `shows.lines` must hold in outline (a bumper's first line is its segment's word; an SOT super has two or three lines, the third starting a recognised label; a brief strip has exactly one line), because that is what the text lints (ticket 6) need to hold against the rundown. It does not say what the rest of a graphic's own text, or a locator's geography, looks like - that is this ticket's own job to invent, draw, and write down, since no earlier ticket did:

- **Bumper.** `shows.lines` is `[word, week_line]`, and for the Echo's bumper only, a third line, the year (`["THE ECHO", "ISSUE 35   16 TO 21 NOV 2026", "1962"]`). `data.bumper_spec` enforces the segment's word matches and that the year appears if and only if the segment is `echo`.
- **Locator.** The shot carries its own `locator` object, sitting beside `shows` rather than inside it (no schema field exists for it yet, and `SHOWS_KEYS` would refuse an unknown key inside `shows`): `{"places": [{"name", "lat", "lon"}, ...] (1 or 2), "scale_km", "push_s": [start, end]}`. `shows.lines` still names each place, in the same order, verbatim - so the subtitle track and the graphic agree without a second, hand-kept copy of the place names, and `data.locator_spec` checks the two match.
- **SOT super / brief strip.** No shot field beyond `shows.lines`, already fully specified by ticket 1's schema.

These are first-draft conventions, not bible text, and the next ticket to touch a v2 screenplay (rundown/2's writer step, or ticket 6's lints) should treat them as what they are: documented here so a builder reading `data.py` is not left guessing why `shows.lines` has two entries for a bumper, and free to supersede them with a decision of its own if the rundown/2 schema wants to carry this differently (e.g. hoisting `locator.places` to a top-level rundown field once a story's parties are known there too).
